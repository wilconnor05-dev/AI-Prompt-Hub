Which AI research tool should you use?

AI research tools are becoming more specialized. Some are better for finding information on the web, while others are designed to analyze your own documents or help with academic literature.

The best choice depends on what kind of research you are doing.

04

Search academic literature

RESEARCH TIP

Always check the sources.

AI research tools can save a huge amount of time, but an AI-generated answer should not automatically be treated as a verified fact.

Open the important sources, check the original material, and compare conflicting claims before using information in important work.
